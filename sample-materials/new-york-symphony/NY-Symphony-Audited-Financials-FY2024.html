Note 4 — Endowment and Spending Policy

The Symphony’s endowment consists of donor-restricted endowment funds and funds designated by the Board to function as endowment (quasi-endowment). Net assets associated with endowment funds are classified and reported based on the existence or absence of donor-imposed restrictions, in accordance with New York’s Prudent Management of Institutional Funds Act (NYPMIFA).

The Board has approved a spending policy under which the Symphony appropriates for distribution each year 5.0% of the trailing twelve-quarter average fair value of the endowment. For the year ended June 30, 2024, the appropriation was approximately $9.0 million. The endowment is invested in a diversified portfolio of equity and fixed-income funds and a limited allocation to alternative investments, managed under the Symphony’s Investment Policy Statement.
