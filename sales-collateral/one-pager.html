Reve
The agentic AI layer for institutional investing

A unified, agentic system for institutional investment management.

Reve is building the AI-native platform where allocation, risk, manager diligence, monitoring, reporting, and operations connect as one intelligent system — with agents that work alongside your team as analysts.

On that foundation, we deploy agents that research managers, stress-test portfolios, draft IC memos, monitor underlying holdings, and model look-through exposures — giving you a new level of visibility, insight, and efficiency across your portfolio, while your team remains in control of every decision. Our team has built investment platforms at Addepar, Opto, and PDT Partners; we know the domain, and we ship production-grade investment software.

What the agents do

Asset allocation & risk modeling
Capital market assumptions, factor models, portfolio optimization, scenario analysis, and cash flow and liquidity projections.
Manager tracking & selection
Continuous manager tracking, investment due diligence, and manager selection based on your evaluation criteria.
Continuous portfolio monitoring
Real-time performance and risk attribution, drift detection, and proactive rebalancing signals.
Memos, reporting & research
Drafting IC memos and board materials, generating stakeholder reporting, and answering ad-hoc research requests on demand.
